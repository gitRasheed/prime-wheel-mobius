Diagnostic answering `IDEAS.md` known-unknown #1: the eq:HN left side
`|Σ_{r mod 𝒬_k} b_k(r) D_N(r/𝒬_k)|` has never been computed or plotted
against `(L_k+N)^{1/2}`; feasibility in k was itself unknown.

## Method (declared before running)

Definitions taken verbatim from the paper
(`paper/seeded_prime_comb_harmonic_reduction.tex`): raw comb
`σ_k = −∏_{p≤y_k} u_p`, `y_k = ⌊√W_k⌋`; smooth core `c_k = σ_k·s_k`;
period `Q_k = ∏_{p≤y_k} p²`, lift `𝒬_k = m_k·Q_k`, `m_k = ⌊W_k/Q_k⌋+1`;
joint field `F_k = a_k^↑ − 2c_k^0` on `Z/𝒬_k`; DFT normalized `1/𝒬`;
`b_k(r) = F̂_k(r)·e(r(L_k+1)/𝒬_k)`; `D_N(θ) = Σ_{j=0}^{N−1} e(jθ)`.

Three parts:

1. **Maximal profile, k = 2..8 (exact integers).** By the kernel-proved
   pairing identity (`spectralPrefix_lower_add_eq_dirichletPrefix`,
   `primorialWheel_residual_eq_moebiusInterval`), the eq:HN left side
   *equals* `R_k(L_k+N) = M(L_k+N) − M(L_k)`. Sieve μ to `W_8 = 9,699,690`,
   compute every `R_k(L_k+N)`, record running-max envelope and the sup
   ratios against both `(L_k+N)^{1/2}` (the pinned eq:HN normalization)
   and `N^{1/2}` (the in-block normalization). Cross-check max|R_k| and
   maximizing x against the paper's Section-7 table.
2. **Spectral-side verification, k = 2,3 (complex float64).** Build
   `b_k(r)` by direct DFT (𝒬₂ = 8, 𝒬₃ = 900), evaluate
   `Σ_r b_k(r)D_N(r/𝒬_k)` for every `1 ≤ N ≤ H_k`, and confirm it
   reproduces the exact integer `R_k` to float precision (max abs error
   reported). Parseval check; raw-comb ℓ¹ identity check
   `Σ_r|σ̂| = ∏ 2(p−1)(2p−1)/p²`; conductor-grouped energy of `b_3`
   (partial first look at known-unknown #2); top coefficients.
3. **Feasibility table, k = 2..8.** Exact `𝒬_k` (digit counts), settling
   which k admit dense spectral computation.

## Results (run 2026-08-09, ~7 s; see `summary.md` and CSVs/SVGs)

**Part 1 — pinned maximal profile, exact, k = 2..8.** All seven
`(max|R_k|, maximizing x)` pairs match the paper's Section-7 table
exactly (independent reproduction from a fresh sieve), and
`Σ_k R_k(W_k) = M(W_8) = 278` reconciles. The eq:HN quantity, in its own
pinned normalization:

| k | sup_N |R_k|/(L_k+N)^{1/2} | sup_N |R_k|/N^{1/2} |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.894 | 1.155 |
| 3 | 0.555 | 1.000 |
| 4 | 0.513 | 1.000 |
| 5 | 0.427 | 1.809 |
| 6 | 0.457 | 1.393 |
| 7 | 0.529 | 1.387 |
| 8 | 0.442 | 1.500 |

The pinned ratio is bounded by 0.9 and non-increasing overall; the
in-block ratio sits at 1.0–1.8 with no trend. In range, eq:HN holds with
room to spare even at ε = 0, C = 1. Finite-range-deception caveat
applies in full: seven blocks over one decade of scale distinguish
nothing asymptotic (declared obstacle; the paper's own status box).

**Part 2 — spectral side verified.** At k = 2 (𝒬 = 8) and k = 3
(𝒬 = 900): exact embedding `F_k(n) = μ(n)` on `1..W_k` confirmed
integer-exactly; the full Dirichlet pairing
`Σ_r b_k(r)D_N(r/𝒬_k) = R_k(L_k+N)` reproduced for **every** N in the
block, max abs error 8.9e-16 (k=2), 1.1e-14 (k=3); Parseval to 3.4e-13;
the raw-comb ℓ¹ identity `Σ_r|σ̂| = ∏_p 2(p−1)(2p−1)/p²` exact to float
precision (1.5 at k=2, 9.6 at k=3). The two coordinate conventions
(paper ↔ this implementation) agree.

**k = 3 landscape (first look at known-unknown #2, toy scale).** ℓ²
energy concentrates at small conductors — q = 6 and q = 30 alone carry
≈ 50% of total energy 0.61; top single coefficients are |b(150)| =
|b(750)| = 4/15 at conductor 6. ℓ¹ mass *anti*-concentrates: the largest
per-conductor ℓ¹ block is the full conductor q = 900 (240 frequencies of
individually tiny mass, ℓ¹ ≈ 1.37) — the smooth-core correction spreads
low-amplitude mass across all conductors. One toy k; not evidence of an
asymptotic profile.

**Part 3 — feasibility settled (the journal's open question).** Dense
spectrum: k ≤ 3 only (𝒬₃ = 900). k = 4: 𝒬₄ = 901,800,900 — dense DFT
marginal, structured route (CRT tensor for the raw part × explicit
44-term smooth set) viable. k ≥ 5: never — 𝒬₅ has 36 digits, 𝒬₈ has
2,638 digits. Consequence: numerical access to eq:HN at growing k is
**permanently via the kernel-proved identity** (exact μ-side, bounded by
sieve range, here W_8 ≈ 9.7×10⁶), not via the Fourier side. Any
attack-angle viability gate that assumes "check the spectrum numerically
at large k" is unimplementable and must be reformulated on the identity
side.

**Verdict.** deployment_status: n/a (diagnostic); candidate_status:
retained. Promotes nothing, closes nothing. Feeds A1–A5 (the pinned
profile is slack in range — the difficulty is genuinely asymptotic, no
finite anomaly to exploit or explain), seeds known-unknown #2's k = 4
structured follow-up, and answers known-unknown #1.
