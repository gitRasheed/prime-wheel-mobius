Phase 1 reconnaissance on the canonical synthesis target. The route
doc (`mobius-synthesis/CURRENT_PROOF_ROUTE.md` §5–6) asserts, from
unpublished finite diagnostics, that C and E are positively correlated
with the same sign and that this correlation is where the C − 2E
cancellation lives. Nobody's data for this is in any repo. This
diagnostic reproduces it independently and maps the landscape the
attack angles will need.

## Method (declared before running)

1. **Definition extraction (agent, read-only):** from the synthesis
   Lean source, the exact computational content of
   `primorialMinimalWheelSystem k` (modulus Q_k), `primorialBlockLower/
   Upper`, `squarePrefixEndpoint`, the corrected residual R_k, the
   zero-mode centering operator `primorialSquareZeroModeCenter`,
   `primeSievePNTCorrectedAllPlusMass`, `primeSievePNTError`, and the
   PNT cutoff `primorialPNTPrimeSieveCutoff` — turned into a numeric
   spec with no interpretation drift.
2. **Computation (EC2, under telemetry):** Möbius sieve to W_9 =
   223,092,870 (numpy, exact integer μ). For every synchronized block
   k ≥ 2 and every complete-square sample X_n in range: exact
   H_{k,n} via the kernel-proved elimination identity; exact/high-
   precision C_{k,n}, E_{k,n} per the extracted definitions (Li terms
   are real-valued — mpmath/float128 with declared error bounds);
   cross-check H = C − 2E at every sample (the implementation gate).
3. **Analyses:** |H| vs √X_n scaling profile; C/E correlation
   (Pearson + sign-agreement rate) per block and pooled; reciprocal-d
   family decomposition of E (which d-ranges carry mass/cancellation);
   all artifacts CSV + SVG.
