Declared before results. Upstream reports the 27-state (squarefree
three-slot) transition matrix is "approximately uniform" and external
framing claims RH is "structurally forced" if parity cancels through it.
Upstream's own route doc §6 already rules a Markov/uniformity assumption
out of any proof route. This record replicates the measurement
independently and measures what the diagnostic actually shows.

## Design

1. Compute S_k = (μ(4k+1), μ(4k+2), μ(4k+3)) for k < K, K up to 10^8/4
   (sieve μ in blocks; memory-bounded).
2. Transition counts T[s→t] over successive four-cells; restrict to the
   8 all-nonzero sign states AND the full 27-state chain separately.
3. Report: (a) max/min cell deviation from uniform vs K; (b) the RATE:
   fit |T_K − U| ~ K^{−α} — α ≈ 1/2 is consistent with square-root
   cancellation folklore, slower is a red flag for the "violently
   annihilates" reading; (c) the degree-one projections W_a, W_b, W_c
   growth exponents vs K^{1/2}; (d) second-order autocorrelations the
   Markov picture would forbid (Chowla-adjacent pair correlations of
   consecutive cells beyond one step).
4. Numerics falsify, never prove: outcomes are SUPPORTS-DIAGNOSTIC /
   REFUTES-UNIFORMITY / REFUTES-MARKOV, each with effect sizes. No
   promotion claim in any outcome.

## Findings

1. **SUPPORTS-DIAGNOSTIC (restricted statistic, with rate).** The 8×8
   all-nonzero sign-pair distribution converges to uniform with TV
   0.0715 → 0.00141 across three decades; fitted rate K^{−0.488} ≈ K^{−1/2}.
   The upstream observation replicates exactly — and the rate is the
   CLT/square-root rate, i.e. what any bounded-discrepancy statistic does.
   It is evidence of square-root-type behavior, not of a mechanism beyond it.
2. **REFUTES-MARKOV.** Conditional mutual information I(s_k; s_{k+2} | s_{k+1})
   plateaus at ≈ 0.248 nats, flat from K = 10^6 to 2.5×10^7 (finite-sample
   bias ≈ 4×10^−4 at the final checkpoint — negligible). The three-slot
   chain has large, persistent two-step memory: it is NOT first-order
   Markov, quantitatively and stably.
3. **REFUTES-UNIFORMITY (full chain).** TV distance of the 27-state joint
   pair distribution from the product of its marginals is 0.1350 and
   CONSTANT (rate exponent −0.012 over three decades): consecutive cells
   converge to a fixed non-product joint law. The zero sector carries
   deterministic correlations (e.g. μ(4k+2) = −μ(2k+1); shared small-prime
   square patterns across neighboring cells), so "mixing to uniform on the
   cube" is false at the 27-state level as a limit statement.
4. max |M(4K)|/√(4K) stays in [0.32, 0.41], flat — unremarkable RH-scale
   behavior in range.

## Reading

The upstream route doc §6 is vindicated by measurement: the transition
matrix is a diagnostic; a Markov/uniformity assumption is not available
(items 2–3 falsify it directly), and the restricted uniformity that does
hold arrives at exactly the square-root rate — it is a symptom of the
target, not a mechanism for it. The "structurally forced" framing should
not survive contact with finding 2. Route A's involution + global charging
problem remains the actual content, as the route doc already says.
