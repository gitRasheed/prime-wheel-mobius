## Results

1. **Identity exact at scale:** E = S − M(K)R(y) closes at every
   sample, max residual 8.4e-11 (float rounding); the boundary
   argument y_k is exactly right (nearby candidates fail by O(1)+).
2. **Decisive: √K-cancellation holds.** C_rms = rms|S|/√(ΣR²) flat at
   0.35–0.43 in every block; in-range slopes −0.14..−0.02, and
   |slope| < 0.012 over 2.5 further decades of K′. Not a d=1
   artifact (participation ratio 123–313 terms).
3. **Abel beats the original form:** μ-weighted coefficient flat;
   M-weighted coefficient grows with K (+0.055..+0.080 and rising per
   block) — the reformulation is empirically the right coordinate.
4. Scales: rms|S|/√x = 0.10–0.18 falling; |S|/x^{3/4} falls ≈ x^{-1/4}
   (triangle loss grows as a power, overshoot ~50–230×); boundary
   term ≪ √x self-consistently. Standard finite-range caveat; nothing
   promotes.
