### Core fatals (cross-confirmed by ≥2 independent reviewers)

1. **The program's central claim is false as stated.** "Kernel chain
   reduces RH to ONE estimate, the Abel face" — no kernel arrow from
   the Abel face to NonzeroResponseRHScale/RH exists (the Abel-face
   files and the bridge files are disjoint; grep-verified). The
   "two kernel-equivalent faces" language shipped in the sealed
   packets of blind rounds 2 and 3 (results/017, results/026).
2. **A dropped second obligation.** The Abel identity's boundary term
   M(K)·R(y) is RH-strength at scale √x for both factors; the
   reduction narrative dropped it. Bounding S alone does not bound M.
3. **Domain disjointness.** Collapse/centering theorems require
   x < y²; the canonical pin lives at x₀ = (y+1)²−1 ≥ y². No (y,x)
   satisfies both — the two kernel halves never compose as narrated.
4. **Record 024's headline is arithmetically false.** At the pin with
   H ≍ y the window is W ≍ log x, not √x (the record evaluates its
   own min backwards); the promised affine slope A_y = 2+o(1) was
   never formalized (module carries only the crude C·(t+1),
   C ≍ √x/log x); declared item 4 (abstract affine lemma) missing; no
   backward-window nonemptiness certificate.
5. **I1's rung-1 hypothesis is RH in disguise.** The μ² diagonal of
   the k=1 Gram form is sign-blind and of size Y^{2+4θ}: E₂(Y)≪Y^{4+ε}
   is equivalent to RH-on-average. Pilot: D(Y) ~ Y^3.74, E₂/D → 0.17.
6. **I2's DC/AC isolation is circular.** |V₀ − A_q| ≪ q^{1+ε}
   unconditionally (record 023's affine modulus already gives O(q)),
   so bounding A_q ⟺ bounding V₀ at every rung-1 exponent — the exact
   pnt-reciprocal-coordinate-change failure mode. Pilot:
   |V₀−A_q| ~ q^0.60. Also: c_q(n) is NOT a Ramanujan sum (naming
   hazard); the spectral-gap step assumes 025's reopening condition.
7. **Upstream's §7 bar is itself overclaimed.** Their measured saving
   is x^{0.678−0.51} = D^{0.336} — materially less than the full
   √-cancellation their own bar demands, and slightly below their
   stated required x^{0.18}.
